⚔ Game Overview

Monster Ghost Army Cards is an epic deck-building battle game for 2-8 players. Command your army of fantastical creatures and ghosts as you clash against your opponents in strategic combat.

Each player builds their army by drafting cards, then battles until only one player remains standing. Victory requires careful selection during drafting and clever tactical decisions during battle.

✎ Drafting Phase

Before battle begins, each player must assemble their army of 10 cards through a drafting process.

  1. Random Order: At the start of each draft round, a random selection order is determined for all players.
  2. Choose One Card: When it's your turn, select one available card from the shared pool. This card joins your army.
  3. 10 Rounds: The drafting continues for 10 rounds, with each player selecting one card per round.
  4. Build Your Army: After all rounds, each player will have exactly 10 cards in their army.
Drafting Strategy

Consider element variety when drafting! Having multiple elements gives you flexibility against different opponents. Also watch what your opponents are drafting to counter their strategies.

⚔ Battle Phase

Once drafting is complete, the battle for supremacy begins!

  1. Turn Order: At the start of battle, a random turn order is established. This order remains the same for all subsequent rounds.
  2. Select Attacker: On your turn, choose one of your remaining cards to be the attacker.
  3. Select Target: Choose any card from any other player to attack.
  4. Choose Attack: Select which attack move to use from your attacker's available attacks.
  5. Defense Response: The targeted player may choose a defense move (if available) to reduce damage.
  6. Apply Damage: Calculate final damage and apply it to the target's health.
Card Elimination

When a card's health reaches 0 or below, it is eliminated from the game and returned to the deck. Lost cards cannot be recovered!

🛡 Defense Moves

Defense moves work opposite to attacks in terms of element interactions:

  • Same Element: Defense blocks the base protection amount
  • Opposite Elements: Attack/defense pairings increase damage by 25%
  • Complementary Elements: Attack/defense pairings reduce damage by 25%
Strategic Defense

Choosing the right defense against specific attacks can significantly reduce incoming damage. Match your defense element to complement the attacker's element for maximum protection!

✦ Special Moves

Special moves are powerful abilities with limited uses. Use them wisely!

Black Hole

An incredibly powerful attack that devastates the target. Cannot be bounced back.

Uses: (Number of Players - 2)

Summon Ghost Army

Call forth spectral warriors to overwhelm your opponent. Cannot be bounced back.

Uses: (Number of Players - 2)

Bounce Back

Reflects 75% of incoming damage back to the attacker. Cannot reflect Black Hole or Summon Ghost Army attacks.

Uses: 2 per card

Teleport

Completely dodges an incoming attack. The attack misses entirely and deals no damage.

Uses: 1 per card

Slime Cover

Cover spikes with slime, especially effective against Mecha attacks. Blunts the attack AND reduces that attack's efficacy by 75% for the rest of the game.

Special ability
Limited Uses

Once a special move's uses are exhausted, it becomes unavailable for that card for the rest of the game. Plan carefully!

🏆 Winning the Game

  1. Card Elimination: When a card's health reaches 0, it is eliminated from the game.
  2. Player Elimination: When a player loses all their cards, they are out of the game.
  3. Victory: The last player remaining with at least one card wins the game!
Victory Tips
  • Draft a balanced army with multiple elements
  • Target opponents' powerful cards early
  • Save special moves for critical moments
  • Use element advantages to maximize damage
  • Protect your strongest cards with smart defense
